# CREO growth kit

How to turn the 30-day cohort into a repeatable way to find creators: prove it on a small group, give everyone a free taste, convert to the cohort, show the proof every Friday.

| File | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `01-pilot-plan.md` | The 10-creator AI and tech pilot: who, how long, what we measure, the week-by-week timeline |
| `02-recruit-dm.md` | Who qualifies, where to find them, the DM and the follow-up |
| `03-permission-and-baseline.md` | The permission message, what becomes public, the baseline request |
| `04-friday-scoreboard-post.md` | The weekly proof post (X thread and image) and the checklist before it goes out |
| `05-x-reply-playbook.md` | Finding people who ask "what should I post?" and replying with one useful idea |
| `06-utm-and-tracker.md` | Tagged links and the two sheets that track everything |
| `07-agency-later.md` | When to start the agency pitch, and what to prepare. Nothing to do yet |
| `templates/*.csv` | Column headers for the creator sheet and the weekly results sheet |

## Rules that apply to everything here

- **Manual only.** No auto-posting, no bots, no scheduled replies, no mass DMs.
- **Nothing is invented.** Every number on a post, card or page comes from a screenshot a creator sent. Unknown results stay as [blanks].
- **Permission first.** A creator's handle, post links and numbers are shown publicly only after they agree in writing (see `03`). They can withdraw any time and we remove it the same day.
- **Say who we are.** Replies and DMs say "I'm building CREO". No pretending to be a fan.
- **No personal data in git.** Names, contacts and screenshots live in the sheets, not in this repo. Only the column headers are here.
- **Claims stay inside what the site says.** The cohort is ₹499 for 30 days, limited seats, a strategist works alongside CREO, progress is shared on days 7, 14, 21 and 30, you approve every post. No promise of results.
