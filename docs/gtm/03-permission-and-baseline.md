# Permission and baseline

## What becomes public, only with a yes
- Their handle.
- Links to the posts they chose to share.
- View counts for those posts and the change against their own baseline.
- Optionally, a quote.

Nothing else. Contacts, screenshots and notes stay private.

## The permission message (version 1)
Send in a DM or email, and keep the reply. A screenshot of their "yes" is the record.

> Before we start, here is exactly what we'd like your OK for:
>
> 1. Showing your @handle and the view counts of posts you've shared with us, compared with your own usual views, in CREO's weekly results posts and on trycreosi.com.
> 2. Linking to a post of yours if it did unusually well.
>
> You can say no to either one, and you can withdraw any time by messaging me. I'll remove you the same day. Anything already posted on X can be deleted on request.
>
> Reply "yes to 1", "yes to 2", "yes to both" or "no".

## How to record it
In the creator sheet, per creator: the scope they agreed to (1, 2, both, none), the date and time of their reply, where the screenshot is saved, and the wording version ("v1"). If they withdraw, add the date and remove them from the next post.

## The baseline request
> To compare fairly, please send screenshots of the views on each of your posts from the last 30 days (a screenshot of the insights page per post is fine, or one list if your app has it). I'll work out your usual numbers from that.

Work out the median in the sheet and write down the date range and how many posts it covers.

## Weekly request (Day 7 after each post)
> Could you send a screenshot of the views on this week's posts, and tell me which one was the CREO suggestion, whether you posted it as it was or changed it, or skipped it?
