# Recruiting: who qualifies, where to look, what to send

## Qualifies if all are true
- Posts about AI or tech (tools, workflows, gadgets, software).
- About 5K to 30K followers on the platform they post most.
- Posts short vertical video (Reels or Shorts) at least once a week. CREO drafts short-video hooks and shot lists, so a creator who only writes text threads is not a fit yet.
- Can send screenshots of views for their last 30 days of posts.
- Will send view screenshots each week for 4 weeks.
- Fine with the permission terms in `03`.

## Skip if
- Under 18, or you cannot tell.
- A brand or agency account, not a person.
- Mostly reposts of other people's videos.
- A competitor's team.

## Where to find about 20
- X search and lists: AI and tech creators who also post Reels or Shorts.
- Instagram: hashtags like #aitools #techreels #chatgpt; read their last 12 posts to check cadence.
- Creator communities and Discords where AI creators share work. Read each community's rules first and follow them.
- Replies on the `05` playbook: people who answer your reply and fit the checklist.

Log every candidate in the creator sheet (`templates/creators.csv`) the day you contact them.

## The DM (version A)
> Hi [first name], I'm [your name], building CREO. It's a 30-day cohort where we suggest what to post next for creators like you, based on what works for your audience. I like your [specific post] on [topic].
>
> We're running a small pilot with 10 AI and tech creators and I'd like you to be one. You'd get a suggested post idea each week, and we'd compare your views against your own usual numbers. Nothing is shown publicly without your written OK.
>
> Interested? I can send the details.

## The DM (version B, shorter)
> Hi [first name], I'm [your name] from CREO. We're testing weekly post ideas with 10 AI and tech creators and comparing views with each person's own baseline. Your [specific post] is why I'm writing. Want the details?

## Follow-up after 3 days (once)
> Hi [first name], no pressure. If you'd like the details of the CREO pilot, say yes and I'll send them. If not, no worries and thanks for the great content.

## If they say yes
Send the link `https://trycreosi.com/cohort#apply` with tags from `06`, then the permission and baseline message from `03`.

## Objections
- "Is it free?" Say exactly what the seat costs or whether it is free, and that a free seat will be disclosed in public posts.
- "Will it work?" "We don't know yet, that's what the pilot is for. No promise of results."
- "Do you see my account?" "No. CREO doesn't connect to your accounts. You send screenshots."
