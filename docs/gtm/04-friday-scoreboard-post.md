# The Friday scoreboard post

Posted every Friday from the second week of the pilot. It covers posts that are now 7 days old.

## Checklist before posting
- [ ] Every creator shown has agreed to scope 1 (and 2 if a post is linked), and has not withdrawn.
- [ ] Every number comes from a screenshot in the sheet. Nothing rounded up.
- [ ] Weeks where the creator skipped the suggestion or posted their own are included in the counts.
- [ ] Creators who went down are in the totals (name them only if they agreed).
- [ ] Any free or discounted seat is disclosed.
- [ ] The caveat line is on it.

## The thread (X)
1. **Headline.** "CREO pilot, week [n]: [x] of [N] creators posted a suggestion. Median change in views vs their own usual: [value]. [up] up, [down] down. Total views on the week's posts: [number]." Add the caveat at the end.
2. **Who.** One line per consenting creator: "@handle: suggested [idea], posted [as is / adapted / skipped], views [number] vs usual [number]."
3. **What did not work.** One honest line: a down week, a skipped suggestion, or something we got wrong.
4. **What went viral** (only with scope 2): the post link and the number.
5. **Caveat line, always:** "Individual results, not a controlled test. Views can move for many reasons. No promise of results."
6. **Close.** "Want a free taste? [link to /preview]. The 30-Day Founding Cohort is ₹499 for 30 days, limited seats: [link to /cohort]."

## The image
One screenshot or a simple card: the headline numbers on top, one row per creator below. Keep it plain. No fake charts.

## What not to do
- Do not name anyone without the "yes".
- Do not compare against other creators. Only against their own baseline.
- Do not delete a bad week to make the story better.
