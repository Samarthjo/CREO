# Finding people who ask "what should I post?" and helping them

Goal: be genuinely useful to one person at a time. A good reply earns a profile visit; a spammy one costs trust.

## Rules
- Manual replies only. Never automate, schedule or bulk reply.
- One reply per thread. No follow-up pings.
- Give the idea first. The link comes after, once.
- Say who you are in your profile or the reply ("I'm building CREO").
- Daily cap: 10 replies. Stop earlier if the quality drops.
- Skip: brands, big accounts, anyone clearly under 18, threads that are complaints about a specific platform's bugs, competitor threads.
- Never claim you looked at their account. You didn't.

## Search queries (X)
Run each, sort by Latest, scan the last 24 hours.
- `("what should I post" OR "what do I post" OR "what to post") (AI OR tech OR ChatGPT OR tools) -is:retweet -is:reply lang:en`
- `("views are down" OR "views dropped" OR "views tanked" OR "reach dropped" OR "views are low") (reels OR shorts OR tiktok) -is:retweet lang:en`
- `("run out of ideas" OR "out of content ideas" OR "content ideas") (creator OR reels OR shorts) -is:retweet lang:en`
- `("why are my views down" OR "why is my reach down") -is:retweet lang:en`
In creator communities, search the same phrases and follow each community's rules about links and self-promotion.

## How to make the reply
1. Read their post. Note their niche if they say it.
2. Open the free preview: `trycreosi.com/preview`. Answer the three questions as if you were them (niche they mentioned, a goal that fits, a format that fits).
3. Pick ONE of the three ideas that suits their post. Screenshot just that idea card.
4. Reply:

> For [niche they mentioned], one idea: "[hook with blanks]". Why it fits: [one line from the card]. I'm building CREO. This is a free starter-ideas tool, picked by niche, goal and format. It doesn't look at your account: [tagged link]

5. If they reply, answer like a person. If they are a fit for the pilot, use the DM from `02`.

## Log it
One row per reply in the tracker (`06`): date, thread link, niche, which idea, whether they replied.

## What a good week looks like
About 30 to 50 replies, a handful of replies back, a few profile follows, and a small number of people trying the preview. Read the numbers from the tracker and from the tagged links; do not guess.
