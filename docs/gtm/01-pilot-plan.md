# Pilot plan: 10 AI and tech creators

## The goal
Show, with real numbers from people the same size as the audience we want, whether CREO's suggestions move a creator's views. The result is the proof for the Friday posts, the cohort page and, later, agencies.

## Who
10 active creators in one niche: **AI and tech**, about 5K to 30K followers, who post short vertical video (Reels or Shorts) at least weekly. Recruit about 20 conversations to land 10. See `02-recruit-dm.md`.

## What we measure
- **Baseline:** the median views per post across the creator's previous 30 days of posts, all posts, taken before they start. They send a screenshot of each post's views.
- **Weekly median:** the median views per post in the week, counted 7 days after each post goes live so platforms are compared fairly.
- **Total views:** the sum of views on that week's posts.
- **Change:** weekly median divided by baseline. Report the median of everyone's change, how many are up and how many are down, never the average (one viral post would skew it).
- **What CREO suggested vs what was posted:** for each week, the suggested idea, and whether it was posted as is, adapted, skipped, or the creator posted something of their own. Skipped and own posts are logged too; without them the comparison is not fair.
- "Went viral" means at least 5 times that creator's baseline. Only shown with permission.

## Timeline
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 0 | DM about 20 creators. Send the permission message and collect baseline screenshots. Confirm 10. |
| Week 1 | Cohort starts. Weekly suggestion sent to each creator. They post and send view screenshots 7 days later. |
| Week 2, Friday | First scoreboard post: week 1 results (see `04`). |
| Weeks 3 and 4 | Same loop. Progress shared with creators on days 7, 14, 21 and 30. |
| Day 30 | Wrap-up message: final numbers, what they want to keep, ask for a quote (only with permission). |

## Fair-play notes (also say these in public)
- Views going up is not proof CREO caused it. Say "not a controlled test".
- Show creators who went down and weeks nobody posted.
- Small group: show "7 of 10" style counts next to any percentage.

## Before the agency phase
See `07-agency-later.md`. Do not start it until the pilot has real, permitted results.
