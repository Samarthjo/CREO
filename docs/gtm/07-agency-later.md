# Creator agencies: later

Not started. This page only says when to start and what to prepare.

## Start when
- The pilot has finished or is past week 3, and
- at least 3 creators have agreed to be shown, and
- the results are honest enough to say out loud, including the weak weeks.

## Prepare then
- A one-page tailored sample for one of the agency's creators: three post ideas made with `/preview` for that creator's niche, labelled as a sample.
- A list of 20 small creator agencies. Columns: agency, size, niches, a person to contact, why they fit, date contacted, reply.
- The offer: a multi-creator pilot first. A team plan only after the workflow has proven useful to them.

## Do not
- Show an agency numbers that are not yet permitted public results.
- Promise results.
